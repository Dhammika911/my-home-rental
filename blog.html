Cracks, Collapses and a National Building Code

Feb 26, 2020

Structural engineering plays a crucial role in ensuring the safety and stability of buildings by analyzing environmental, geotechnical, and structural factors. It helps identify potential issues like cracks, sloping floors, or foundation settlement early on, reducing long-term risks. Especially in countries like Sri Lanka, where land conditions vary greatly, involving a structural engineer is essential for site-specific analysis and safe design. With increasing construction activity, particularly in cities like Colombo and hilly areas like Kandy, professional input and standardized building codes are more important than ever.

Tips to Purchase the Best Apartments

Feb 26, 2020

Living in an apartment in Colombo can feel restricting at first, especially with space and cost concerns. However, finding the right apartment becomes easier when you know what to look for. Key factors include ceiling height for a spacious feel, a scenic or practical balcony view, and smart storage solutions to manage your belongings. Don’t overlook the layout either—open-plan designs make even small spaces feel larger and more livable. With careful attention to these details, you can find a comfortable, affordable apartment that truly feels like home.

Small Home Interior Designing Ideas

Feb 26, 2020

In a bustling city where spacious homes are rare and pricey, tiny houses have become the go-to choice for many. But with the right design tricks, even the smallest space can feel warm, stylish, and functional. From inbuilt wall shelves for clever storage to revamping old furniture and adding your own DIY decor, there are countless ways to make a small home truly yours. Repainting chairs, layering textiles, and embracing personalized touches help turn compact living into cozy, creative spaces. Small doesn’t have to mean less—it’s all about making it work with style!
